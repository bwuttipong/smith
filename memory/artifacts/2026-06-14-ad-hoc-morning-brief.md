## 🚨 #1 — Fable 5 / Mythos 5 forced offline by US government

- **Tuesday June 9, 2026** — Anthropic released Claude Fable 5, the public version of Mythos 5 that had been running only with Project Glasswing partners (~200 orgs in 15+ countries scanning critical infrastructure codebases — they found 10,000+ high/critical security flaws during preview)
- By every measure, the first true next-gen LLM:
  - Top of Artificial Analysis intelligence index (~5 points ahead of GPT-5.5)
  - ~10T parameters (per circulating estimates, not confirmed by Anthropic)
  - **Long-horizon autonomy** — can run for days without intervention
  - Andrej Karpathy called it "a step change of the same order as Opus 4.5 was in November"
  - Vision lead — first time Anthropic beats OpenAI
  - On par with GPT-5.5 on DeepSWE coding benchmark, beating every Opus score, at lower total cost
- **Friday June 12, evening** — US Commerce Department hit Anthropic with an emergency export control directive. Reason cited: alleged jailbreak / national security concerns. Specific details NOT shared with Anthropic
- Order covered "foreign nationals, whether inside or outside the United States" — so Anthropic had no choice but to pull the model from **every** customer, US and foreign
- **First time the US government has forced a commercial AI product offline**
- Anthropic statement: disputes concerns, calls it a "misunderstanding"
- Anthropic had also been fighting the Trump administration on related issues; reportedly had a designation barring government agencies/contractors from using their tech, and Anthropic filed lawsuits
- **Strategic irony**: Anthropic's CEO had been publicly calling for exactly this kind of oversight on safety grounds. The safety-first positioning may have backfired

## 🧠 The read

The Fable 5 thing changes the game. The US just proved it can pull a frontier model offline. Every AI lab is recalibrating tonight. Anthropic's stock-for-stock moat was always "we have the best model" — and now they don't, because they were forced to kill it. The Mythos-only-with-Project-Glasswing roll-out strategy looked brilliant two days ago (responsible scaling, security partner vetting) and now looks like a preview of what every frontier lab will be forced to do.

Watch for:
- OpenAI's next move (GPT-6 response, almost certainly accelerated)
- The Anthropic lawsuit
- Whether Fable 5/Mythos 5 come back in a nerfed form
- The bigger policy fight: who decides what "exportable" means when a model is also running on US soil
- Bezos's Prometheus is the dark horse — physical-world AI with Bezos-grade patience and balance sheet

The "AI cold war just went live" framing is appropriate. The gap between US and Chinese AI was the strategic question of 2025. The gap between "public" and "deployable" is now the strategic question of 2026.
