Apollo readout:
The week’s theme is not “AI is hot”; that’s stale porridge. The sharper read is that capital is moving into the plumbing: compute, agent security, open models, voice APIs, robotics interfaces, and agent-to-agent networks. Consumer magic demos are less interesting than infrastructure that either lowers cost or stops autonomous systems making a royal mess of the furniture.
