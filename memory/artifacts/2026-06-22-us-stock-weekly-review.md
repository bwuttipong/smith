## Major News Drivers

### 1. **U.S.–Iran Peace Deal — Deflationary Shock** 🕊️
- **Terms:** Immediate ceasefire, Hormuz reopens toll-free, Iran resumes oil exports immediately, formal signing June 19 in Switzerland.
- **Market impact:** WTI crude dropped **$15 in 4 trading days** ($89 → $73.52) — a *structural repricing* of geopolitical risk premium.
- **Implication:** If oil holds near $73 through June, **June CPI (mid-July) could print 2.5–3.0%** vs. May's 4.2% — fundamentally altering Fed calculus.

### 2. **Fed Regime Change: Kevin Warsh's Debut** 🏦
- **First FOMC as Chair (June 16–17).**
- **Hawkish surprise:** Dot plot shifted to hiking bias; forward guidance **abolished**.
- **Communications revolution:** No chair dot plot, five task forces, explicit data-dependency.
- **Market adjustment:** Markets can no longer "know" Fed path — structurally disorienting after 15 years of forward guidance dependence.
- **Silver lining:** Data-dependency means falling oil/softer CPI *can* change September–December outlook.

### 3. **SpaceX: Amazon-in-2000 Behavior** 🚀
- **IPO (June 12):** $135/share, $75B raise, largest ever → closed Day 1 at $160.95 (+19%), valuation >$2.1T.
- **Cursor acquisition (June 16):** $60B all-stock (80% of IPO proceeds) for AI coding tool with $4B ARR.
- **Price action:** $135 → $225.64 intraday (Tue) → $201.80 close (+49.5% in 2 sessions).
- **Bull case:** Vertically integrated AI infrastructure (space logistics + AI coding + Starlink + xAI).
- **Bear case:** CFRA $115 target; Morningstar fair value $63.

### 4. **Apple–Intel Partnership: CHIPS Act's Biggest Bet** 🤝
- **Trump announcement:** *"Apple has agreed to work with Intel to design and build its Chips in America."*
- **Intel:** +10.22% Thursday, market cap ~$609B, **+464% in 12 months** (from ~$19).
- **Significance:** Most prestigious external foundry customer win; validates 18A node & government 10% stake.
- **Key test:** Q2 earnings (July 23) — first look at whether foundry commitments convert to contracted revenue. Yields reportedly 2 quarters ahead of schedule.

## Bottom Line

**A historic, event-packed week that ended net positive.** The Iran peace deal delivered a powerful deflationary impulse that ultimately overwhelmed the Fed's hawkish pivot. Semiconductors led a violent Thursday reversal, small-caps made new highs, and market breadth broadened — all constructive for the bull market's durability. However, **Warsh's "no guidance" regime means higher volatility and less predictability** going forward. The market is now priced for a data-dependent Fed where **every inflation print (especially PCE on Thursday) is a potential regime-shifter**.

> **Risk-on, but with wider error bars.** The peace deal is a genuine positive supply shock; the Fed's hawkishness is a demand-side tightening. The net outcome depends on which force wins — and for now, the oil collapse is winning.
