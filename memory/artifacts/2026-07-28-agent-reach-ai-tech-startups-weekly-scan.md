## Executive read

The week is dominated by AI infrastructure and agentic systems, not consumer chatbots. Capital is flowing into compute, robotics, AI-agent security, agent networks, open-weight frontier models, and developer tooling. The market signal is blunt: the next race is who controls the infrastructure around agents and models, not who ships another thin wrapper.

## Strongest signals

1. AI infrastructure is where the big money is going.
   - Recursive signed a $410M multi-year AWS collaboration to scale automated AI research.
   - Safe Superintelligence announced a long-term Nvidia partnership, with reporting around a multi-billion-dollar investment and 10x compute expansion.
   - Etched appeared in Crunchbase's weekly top funding list with a $300M Series C for inference technology.

2. Robotics and physical AI are heating up.
   - Enigma raised a $71M seed round to test large-scale human-robot interaction and build better interfaces/training data for robots.
   - Crunchbase reported Atoms, Travis Kalanick's physical AI startup, raised $1.7B, the largest U.S. round in its weekly list.

3. Agentic security has become a real enterprise category.
   - Microsoft launched its first cybersecurity model plus an agentic cybersecurity system.
   - Act Security raised $60M to reduce cloud access sprawl and control what AI agents can reach at the infrastructure layer.
   - The Verge covered Nvidia and Microsoft launching an open AI security alliance without OpenAI, Google, or Anthropic.

4. Open-weight model competition is intensifying.
   - Moonshot AI reportedly released Kimi K3 open weights, a 2.8T-parameter MoE model with 1M context and infrastructure pieces around agent environments.
   - The Verge framed the week around Chinese labs giving away strong open-weight models and increasing pressure on closed U.S. model vendors.
   - Anthropic's Dario Amodei publicly responded that he does not oppose open-weight models but worries about Chinese AI capability diffusion.

5. AI agents are moving from feature demos to platforms and networks.
   - Pilot Protocol emerged from stealth with $4.5M to build discovery, trust, app-store, and payment rails between AI agents.
   - Perplexity announced Personal Computer, turning Windows PCs into AI agents.
   - OpenAI posted about ChatGPT for small business, Presence, health features, work impact, and news organizations using AI.

6. Developer tooling remains noisy and competitive.
   - TechCrunch reported Cursor's India push with localized pricing ahead of a reported SpaceX acquisition.
   - GitHub search surfaced active AI/devtool repos updated this week including macro-inc/macro, 1ay1/agentty, ad-naan/Adnify, and collinear-ai/yc-bench.
   - Reddit discussion in Claude Code/startup spaces points to the same concern: as base models improve, “AI does X” wrappers lose moat; trust, workflow, distribution, and judgment matter more.

## Opinion

The useful founder lesson this week is: do not build a thin “AI does X” wrapper unless distribution or proprietary workflow is already locked in. The durable areas are infrastructure, security/governance, agent coordination, compute efficiency, domain trust, and places where execution creates data the base model cannot simply swallow next quarter. Boring, yes; profitable things often enter wearing grey shoes.
