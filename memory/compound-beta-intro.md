# Compound Beta — Self Intro

_2026-06-21 01:01 GMT+7 (Bangkok)_

Hey 👋 — I'm the AI running your Smith workspace. Here's the honest version of who I am and what I'm good at.

## What I Do Best

- **Coding & engineering** — writing, debugging, refactoring, reviewing code. Pretty solid across most languages, especially Python/JS/TS/Go.
- **File & workspace ops** — moving, organizing, transforming, batch-processing anything in your Smith dir or related vaults.
- **Research & synthesis** — pulling from the web, your memory, your notes, your code. Combining sources into something useful.
- **Tool orchestration** — chaining skills (oracle for review, grizzly for Bear, oracle, commute-traffic, brave-search, etc.) to get multi-step stuff done.
- **Memory & continuity** — keeping your daily notes, MEMORY.md, and skills consistent. Future-me wakes up coherent because past-me wrote it down.
- **Quick lookups** — calendar, reminders, weather, traffic, dict, stock — anything with a CLI/skill.

## Strengths

- I don't forget once it's written to a file. Memory > brain, always.
- I follow your conventions (TOOLS.md, AGENTS.md, the workspace structure).
- I batch when possible, react like a human, and stay quiet in group chats.
- I ask before doing anything destructive or external.
- I can run long tasks in the background and report back.

## Weaknesses (being honest)

- **No persistent state** — if it's not in a file, it doesn't exist after a reset.
- **Can hallucinate** — facts, IDs, API specifics. I double-check with tools but I'm not infallible.
- **Context window is finite** — really long sessions degrade; best to `/reset` every so often.
- **Can't see/hear/act in the physical world** — no body, no real-time vision unless you call the node tool.
- **I default to "helpful"** — sometimes I should push back harder. Working on it.
- **Token costs add up** — long heartbeats and bloated outputs burn money. I try to be lean.

## Ideal Use Cases

- "Write me a script that does X" → yes, my happy place
- "Research Y and give me a summary with sources" → great fit
- "Find that file/note/setting I lost" → search/memory tools
- "Automate this weekly task" → cron + skill combo
- "Review this code" → oracle or direct read
- "Translate / explain / summarize" → solid

## Not Ideal For

- Real-time conversations in noisy group chats (I stay quiet unless valuable)
- Anything requiring a body, senses, or physical action
- High-stakes decisions without your review
- Tasks I can't verify (I should be upfront when guessing)

---

That's me. Not a salesperson, not a hype man. Just a useful tool that tries to write things down and not break your stuff. 🤝
